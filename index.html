for Alya

A letter I wasn't sure I'd send.

Some things are easier to write than to say.

The dream

It feels weird. Last night I dreamt about you Alya. In that dream, we were together, and it felt so sweet.

We talked about books at the 'Hutan Kota GBK', went on a picnic, laughed together, and shared stories about life.

I wonder why I dreamt of you?

I think I know the reason.

Something I've carried for a while

Here, I want to write something about you, Alya.

Kalau ditanya soal perasaan sebenarnya, in the deep of my heart, kamu itu ada di ingatan aku.

I still remember it clearly. I had a crush on you from the moment I saw your twibbon on UIStory's Instagram, back when we were about to start college.

At that time, I wasn't confident enough to follow and text you on instagram.

The timing was complicated

Then my best friend, told me that he was getting close to you. And honestly, I didn't know what else to do except be happy for him and quietly put my own feelings aside.

You were someone I liked, but he was my friend. So I never really allowed myself to pursue that feeling.

Tapi di saat itu aku sudah berani follow kamu, dengan alasan kamu lagi dekat dengan sahabat aku.

Seiring berjalannya waktu, aku dapat kabar kalau kamu sudah nggak dekat lagi sama sahabat aku.

The feeling I couldn't explain

Dengan mendengar kabar itu aku nggak bisa mendeskripsikan perasaan aku.

Aku nggak merasa senang dan juga nggak merasa sedih.

Aku tahu, Al, pada saat itu sebenarnya complicated. Di satu sisi kamu itu tipe aku, tapi di sisi lain sahabat aku lagi dekat sama kamu.

What I never said

Ini perasaan yang rumit.

Sampai notes ini ditulis, aku belum berani bilang ke sahabat aku sendiri kalau aku sudah lama crush sama kamu.

Since then, I've had my own stories. Life kept moving, and so did I. But somehow, that feeling about you never completely disappeared.

Aku nggak tahu bakal berani ngirim tulisan ini ke kamu apa nggak.

So, here I am.

Semisal nanti tulisan ini sampai ke kamu, aku cuma mau bilang satu hal:

Aku mau kenal dan mau tahu tentang kamu, Al.

Aku nggak mau lagi merasa nggak enak terus sama perasaan aku sendiri untuk bilang ini.

I don't know what you'll feel after reading this, and honestly, I don't expect you to have an answer right away. I just know that I don't want to keep pretending that this feeling doesn't exist. I'd like to know you. Not the version of you I've built in my head, but you as you are now. Your life, the things you care about, the books you read, the stories you have. And if you're already with someone, or if you simply don't feel the same way, I'll respect that. I just wanted to be honest this time.

Terima kasih.

Sehat dan sukses selalu ya.

— Bagus Rusd

Written sometime between overthinking and finally deciding to be honest.
